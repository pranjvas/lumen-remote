# Lumen UX pass — 2026-10-23

The owner's verdict: "The app UI is very clunky." This pass is phone-first: an iPhone in Safari or
Chrome at about 390×844 CSS px, and often about 390×664 once the browser toolbars show, on the LAN at
`http://192.168.1.7:8790`. Desktop (1440×900) must not regress.

Method: every view was rendered on `tools/dev-ui.mjs` (fake TV) at 390×844, 390×664 and 1440×900 in
both themes, plus interaction shots (`--extras`), and compared with Apple TV Remote, Sonos, Google Home
and Linear/Things-level polish. Before/after shots are under `%TEMP%/lumen-ux/{before,after}/`.

## Audit: what makes it feel clunky

Ranked by how much each one costs on a phone.

1. **The chrome eats the screen.** The header (60 px), the ask bar (56 px) and an 8-slot tab bar
   (62 px) take about 180 of 844 px (21 %), and about 27 % at 664 px. Lumen AI shows up twice on
   every screen: the header **Ask** pill and an ask bar right above the tabs.
2. **Eight tabs.** Remote · Screen · Apps · Cast · Browser · Settings · Mouse · More: 48 px slots with
   labels that run together ("BrowserSettings"). Mouse is a primary tab while its sibling Touch &
   Text is hidden in More, and the active indicator is a 2 px line that is hard to see.
3. **iOS zooms into every text field.** The ask bar (14.5 px), the address bar, filters and `.field`
   inputs are all under 16 px, so Safari zooms the page on focus, and the user has to pinch back out.
4. **Hover sticks on touch.** 21 `:hover` rules run on iPhone too. After a tap, a button keeps its
   hover background until the next tap somewhere else, so state looks wrong.
5. **Press feedback is uneven.** Keys have a good pressed state, but `.btn`, chips, app tiles, More
   tiles and nav items get only a 1 px nudge or nothing. iPhones have no `navigator.vibrate`, so a
   tap feels like nothing happened. On the Screen view, `navigator.vibrate` also logs an intervention
   warning before the first tap.
6. **Too much badge noise.** Every Settings row carries a boxed green **LIVE** pill, and the
   now-playing strip carries a boxed **TV AGENT** pill. When everything is loud, the exceptions
   (stale, locked, unavailable) stop standing out.
7. **Toasts cover the header.** Phone toasts drop over the now-playing card and the header ("Closed on
   the TV" hides the app name).
8. **View headers are heavy.** A 28 px title plus a full-sentence subtitle and 18 px margins on every
   view repeat what the tab already says and push content down by about 90 px.
9. **The Settings index is broken.** It is a sticky pill that clips its last chip ("Gene…") with no
   scroll cue, and scrolled rows peek out above it.
10. **The Browser view is stacked too deep.** A status line, a VPN line and a "Use a config file…"
    button take three rows above the page. The empty state is a card inside a card with a 24 px
    headline. The tool row is a set of boxed buttons right on top of the tab bar.
11. **The Screen view's controls sprawl.** A status pill, a centred segmented control and two wide
    buttons take three rows between the picture and Steer, so at 664 px Steer is off screen.
12. **Small type.** Labels on the remote cluster are 8.5 px uppercase in `--ink-3`, and other labels
    are 9–9.5 px. Both are below comfortable reading size on a phone.
13. **Header crowding.** The theme toggle sits in the phone header next to Ask, although More already
    has it.

What already works and should stay: the halo d-pad with its live volume arc, the warm tint that
follows the foreground app, Geist and Geist Mono, the glass surfaces, the provenance model, and the
optimistic-with-readback volume.

## Design direction

**Principles**

- *Content first, chrome quiet.* There is one Lumen AI entry point per screen on phones: the header
  Ask pill. The tab bar is short, and nothing duplicates it.
- *Exceptions are loud, normal is quiet.* Live values get a coloured dot, not a boxed pill. Stale,
  locked and unavailable keep their outlines.
- *Every tap answers.* A shared press response (scale 0.97 with a tint wash, 90 ms in and 160 ms
  out) on every tappable surface, plus a haptic tick on iOS 18 (the `<input switch>` technique) and
  `navigator.vibrate` where it exists.
- *Touch is not hover.* Hover styles apply only on `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
- *One scale.* Spacing tokens (`--sp-1…8` = 4/8/12/16/20/24/32/40) and type tokens (`--fs-xs…2xl`).
  Labels are at least 10 px. Inputs are 16 px on touch screens.

**Navigation model (phone)**

Five destinations plus More: **Remote · Screen · Apps · Browser · Settings · More**. More is a sheet
with Cast, Mouse, Touch & Text, Keys, Scenes, Ask Lumen, then the theme and vibration settings. The
active tab gets a tinted pill behind its icon, as in iOS 26 and Material 3. Desktop keeps the full
rail.

**Component and motion system**

- `.btn`, `.chip`, `.icon-btn`, `.app-btn`, `.more-item`, `.nav-item` and `.seg button` share the
  press response. Disabled controls never animate.
- Badges come in two forms: quiet (dot + label, no fill) for live/agent/tracked/derived/reading, and
  outlined for stale/locked/unavailable.
- Toasts rise from above the tab bar on phones and stay in the bottom-right corner on desktop.
- Sheets keep the bottom-sheet pattern, with a larger grab handle and a safe-area bottom.

## Per-view changes, ranked by impact

| # | Where | Change |
|---|-------|--------|
| 1 | Shell | 6-slot tab bar with a pill indicator. Ask bar removed from phone views except Remote on tall screens. Theme toggle moved to More on phones. Header 56 px. |
| 2 | System | 16 px inputs on touch, hover only for fine pointers, shared press response, iOS haptics, spacing and type tokens. |
| 3 | System | Quiet provenance badges. |
| 4 | Shell | Phone toasts above the tab bar. |
| 5 | All views | Compact phone view headers (22 px title, tighter margins). |
| 6 | Settings | Full-bleed frosted sticky index that scrolls sideways with edge fades, so rows never show above it. |
| 7 | Screen | Controls in one row (rate + icon actions). Steer moves up. |
| 8 | Browser (layout only) | One-line VPN status, a flat empty state, a lighter toolbar. |
| 9 | Remote | Readable cluster labels (10 px, `--ink-2`). |

**Not in this pass (proposals)**

- Fold Mouse and Touch into a mode switch on the Remote (D-pad | Touch | Mouse). That needs JS
  restructuring of three views.
- Show Apps' "On screen now" hero only on desktop. The phone already has the Remote's now strip.
- Swipe between tabs and pull-to-refresh on Settings.
- View Transitions API crossfades between tabs (Safari 18 supports same-document transitions).
- An "Edit tabs" option in More, so the owner can pick which five destinations are pinned.
